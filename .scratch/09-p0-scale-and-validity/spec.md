# P0 scale and causal-validity expansion

Status: done

## Issue

The durable 48-actor, 12-hour survival slice is a valid architecture demonstration, not the P0 target of roughly 1,000 actors over decades. Current demo bounds are 256 actors and 240 hours; its Eat/Rest ecology has no birth, death, weather, migration, learning, or institutions. Increasing only the counters would produce misleading claims.

## Objective

Measure the current architecture under its declared maximum, identify CPU/memory/storage and trajectory bottlenecks, then choose the smallest new causal mechanism and validation horizon that move P0 toward a survival society without sacrificing replay, audit, numerical projections, or finite budget.

## Acceptance criteria

- Profile the current 256-actor/240-hour bound with exact commands, outcome metrics, wall/CPU time, memory, and event/journal/checkpoint size.
- Inspect representative early/middle/late actor and world events rather than treating reaching the horizon as validity.
- Identify model pathologies separately from runtime bottlenecks, with a falsifiable next design change.
- Define a bounded next vertical slice and its acceptance/stop gates; do not claim P0 completion from a scale-only run.

Need-review: yes
Need-test-cases: yes

## Blockers

None. Diagnostic evidence and review are complete; successor food-flow pilot is tracked separately.
