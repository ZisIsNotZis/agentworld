## 1. CENTRAL CLAIM — tested anywhere? **No.** (confidence: high)

VISION.md:11-13 states the central claim: selective allocation of high-cost reasoning "preserves civilization-level fidelity better than uniform or periodic model invocation at the same budget."

Repo-wide grep for `http`, `net`, `client`, `prompt`, `infer`, `llm`, `openai`, `anthropic` over `*.go` returns no call site — only comments (e.g. "no LLM call, `token_cost=0`", `.scratch/10-food-flow-pilot/spec.md:96`; "no LLM integration", ticket 07 comment). - **(b) Cognitive-value/attention scheduler: ABSENT.** `internal/scheduler/queue.go:26-34` orders wakes strictly by `(At, Actor, Cause)` — pure time order, no value term. `WakeCause` defines `WakeStrategyInvalidation` and `WakeAudit` (`queue.go:16-27`), but **no code ever schedules a `WakeStrategyInvalidation`** (zero producers), and `WakeAudit` is repurposed as the runner's clock tick (`internal/world/natural_checkpoint.go:406`: `naturalClockActor`). - **(c) Per-intervention cause/cost/patch/result records: ABSENT for cognitive interventions.** What exists is an *action-attempt* journal (actor, action, observed version, time, key, status, reason, event link — P0-SPEC §8/S5), which records deterministic policy attempts, not reasoning interventions. - **(d) Adaptive vs uniform/periodic/random comparison: ABSENT.** `docs/VALIDATION.md:130-133` contains a "Cognitive-allocation experiments" section listing exactly the metrics that should be measured (model calls, tokens, detected failures, macro divergence from a higher-budget reference, calibration error of predicted priority) — it is pure design text with zero runs behind it. The only policy comparisons ever executed are contrasts between *hand-authored behavior policies* at identical 32/32 compute budgets (`natural/5|6|7|7b|9`) and mechanism/fixture ablations (v6/v7, births on/off, cultivation on/off).

**The central claim has never been instantiated in code, let alone tested.** The repo's own docs agree: ROADMAP.md:14 excludes "language-model strategy editing" from P0 and parks the differentiating experiment in P1 ("at equal budget, at least one adaptive scheduler outperforms periodic and random baselines," ROADMAP.md:30) — a phase for which there are zero tickets, zero commits, and zero lines of code.

## 2. AI INTERVENTION / SELF-EVOLUTION — **Absent; ADR promises unimplemented.** (confidence: high)

- **What ADR 0002 promises:** "Language-model managers must update strategies during a run"; strategies "can be inherited, recombined, mutated, tested, shared, and rolled back"; every change records parent, proposer, validation, rollout state (`docs/adr/0002:7-12`). **What ADR 0004 promises:** beam-style evaluation of multiple cognitive schedulers from shared checkpoints.
- **What exists:** strategies are versioned *frozen data* with a `Ref{ID, Version}` and a `ParentRef` that is explicitly "an exact lineage reference, **not implicit inheritance**: actions, defaults and budget are always complete in this policy" (`internal/strategy/policy.go:70-71`). Every policy family is a Go-authored frozen constructor: `FrozenSocialFoodPolicy` is "the declared experiment, **not a learned or**…" (`strategy/socialfood.go:59`); `natural/9` is "the natural/7b cascade VERBATIM plus exactly three wage-contract additions" authored by hand in Go source (`strategy/natural9.go:9-14`). "Inheritance" happens at authoring time, in Go, by humans/agent designers — the runtime has no patch, mutation, sandbox, rollout, or rollback path. The only "mutation" the strategy tests contain are *negative* tests proving wire bytes must not change (`natural9_test.go:310-317`).
- **Online adaptation of behavior: none.** Policies are memoryless at decision time (natural9.go:20-21: "memoryless evaluation" is a *design virtue* recorded in reviews); the only actor-carried state across hours is a fixed set of last-denial/last-hour sentinel fields.
- **Genetics is demographic, not strategic.** Ticket 14's heredity is allele inheritance across births (locus-m allele-7 frequency 0.50→1.00 by h72). This is sampling drift in a small population — no allele is linked to fitness, no selection pressure exists, and no strategy is inherited.
- **Nearest real analogues:** run-level branch forks from checkpoints (flag interventions like births/branch-eat-cost; ADR-0004-style *branching* works), and the `WakeStrategyInvalidation` enum — a named hook for runtime strategy replacement that has never once been produced. The word "escalation" appears in code exactly once — as a review-process comment (`world/natural_contract.go:1279`), not as `EscalationRequest`, which P0-SPEC §2/§10 name and which does not exist as a type.

**Verdict:** ADRs 0002/0004 describe an end-state; the implemented and exercised portion is exactly the half that requires no cognition: immutable versioned data + run branching. The self-evolution half (P11: schemas → sandbox trials → counterfactual evaluation → staged rollout → rollback) is entirely unimplemented.

## 6. OPPORTUNITY COST — what the central claim needed, and the smallest honest experiment (confidence: high on inventory; medium-high on feasibility)

To test "adaptive reasoning allocation beats uniform at equal budget" you need exactly five things: (1) a decision seam where an *expensive decider* can be consulted for a chosen (actor, instant) — the runner already owns every decision instant and calls frozen policy evaluators there; (2) an intervention ledger (cause/cost/patch/result) — the attempt journals are 80% of this; (3) an allocation-policy layer choosing who gets the budget — absent; (4) baselines (periodic, random, uniform, full reference) — trivially expressible as allocation policies; (5) a fidelity metric vs a high-budget reference — the reporters already compute per-cell trajectory/ledger comparisons and would supply this.

**Exists:** kernel with atomic commits and replay; deterministic scheduler; frozen policy evaluation seams per actor-hour; branch/fork machinery from verified checkpoints; per-attempt typed journals; cost fields (CPU/bytes; token slot present but constant); a measured map of where decisions matter (ticket 19's phase map: f=10/11 reliable 8/8, f=12 fragile, f=16 NULL) and a measured decision-sensitivity datum (ticket 18's four gating cells at f=12: natural/7 2/4 vs natural/7b 4/4; ticket 19's eight-seed phase map at f=12: natural/7 2/8 vs natural/7b 7/8 — reachability *is* decision-dependent exactly where the fixture is fragile).
**Missing and blocking:** the paid-decision override interface, the intervention cost ledger, the allocation-policy layer, and multi-seed allocation comparison harness. That is all — nothing essential in the physics/economics/conservation stack is missing for a *stub* experiment.

**Smallest honest experiment:** on the existing `cmd/natural` fixture at f=10-12 (where decisions demonstrably matter), define an oracle with a cost — a clairvoyant bag-decision (e.g., build iff the completion will claim a slot and the climb to k≥2 is arithmetically viable). Arms at equal oracle-call budget per run: full reference (oracle every decision), periodic (every Kth decision), uniform random K decisions, adaptive (spend on actors within ε of a threshold: k=1, energy near TLow, granary==1). Metrics: V8-A2 reachability across seeds 0-7, first-sustained hour, births, extinction, surplus flow. If adaptive > periodic/random at equal budget across seeds, the central claim has its first evidence; if *no* allocation policy differentiates, that is the decisive measurement that the substrate's decision-space is too degenerate to justify any reasoning budget — which is precisely the fact the operator needs to know and which 20 tickets of ecology work could never reveal. No LLM is required; the stub oracle is honest for testing *allocation* (it is not honest for testing *LLM value*, which is a second experiment).

## 7. STEELMAN BOTH SIDES

**Strongest case the path is correct:** the central claim is a claim about *marginal value of reasoning*, and marginal value is undefined in a world without stakes. The project's sequence — make production endogenous (13), make limits natural (15/P15), make surplus reachable (18), measure where it is reliable (19), make exchange possible (20) — is a defensible construction of "real stakes" from nothing. The instrumentation built on the way (byte-frozen versions, predeclared gates, verbatim nulls, worker-invariant digests, 230-bundle exact replay) is exactly the measurement instrument any allocation experiment will need; building it after the LLM arrives would have been impossible. The failures were predeclared as valid outcomes and never tuned — the epistemics are impeccable. And P15's ceiling discipline surfaced a real defect honestly (the harness is 40× too slow at 256 actors), which is information, not drift.

**Strongest case the operator is right:** the central claim concerns the allocation of *cognition*, and after 20 tickets the repo contains zero cognition, zero allocation, zero baselines — while containing ~9,000 lines of frozen-economy fixture and a 115-cell measurement of its phase boundary. Four consecutive tickets falsified designs against an arithmetic identity the designers themselves froze; the correct response to "our world is a tautology" was to widen the decision space (actors, heterogeneity, mobility, uncertainty — the P0 scope items *weather/movement/conflict* that never got built), not to add a density dial. Worse: in the world as originally declared, better decisions cannot change population outcomes at all (every policy → identical sterile extinction; cultivation ablation changes nothing but a ledger), so the substrate was, until ticket 18, structurally incapable of rewarding intelligence — the one property the vision exists to exploit. The evidence discipline, genuine as it is, has been applied with increasing finesse to a question (can our hand-written policies produce surplus in our hand-written economy?) that needed answering once, not eleven times. That is the signature of drift: rigor migrating from the differentiating question to the tractable one.

**My verdict:** the operator is right about the *direction of travel* but the project is not a dead end — it is an over-built instrument with a missing subject. See DRIFT VERDICT below.
