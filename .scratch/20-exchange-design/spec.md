# Exchange and trade (design, v9)

Status: claimed

## Issue

The operator's arc after reproduction/genetics is exchange and trade, then money, then richer social concepts. Until now that arc had no substrate: the ecology produced no surplus, so there was nothing to exchange. Ticket 18 changed that — surplus is reachable at sub-saturation density, the holders are frequently second-generation newborns, and the v4 consent/privacy machinery now runs organically — and ticket 19 is measuring the phase boundary (where surplus is reliable, who holds it, and for how many hours a surplus holder coexists with an alive actor holding nothing) precisely so this design can choose cells where exchange is *possible* rather than hypothetical.

What exists as tradable value today is deliberately thin: food in a bag (capacity 1) and in a granary (spoils on a 24-hour clock), capital points that are **immovable by construction** (possession-by-identity: only the owner's own intentions may touch a worksite, and tenure follows the owner's slots), labour time (one gather and one build per actor-hour, and the frozen auto-feed rule ties a worker's meal to the granary), and land (8 slots per patch, claimed by build completion). There is no fungible token, no transfer of capital, no contracts, no reputation, and no enforcement. Any exchange design must therefore say what is being traded and why both sides want it — a double coincidence that the current goods barely support.

## Operator direction (recorded)

Sequencing: productive capacity → surplus → reproduction/genetics → exchange/trade and money, with affect/motive richness (selfishness, altruism, opportunism) before reciprocity/credit. Principle P15 governs (limits come from modelled nature or our compute, never arbitrary caps); P10 forbids preinstalling modern institutions as necessities; every frozen gate is declared before its runs and never tuned afterwards, and a null result is a valid outcome reported verbatim.

## Objective

Design (not implement) v9, the minimal exchange mechanism that makes voluntary, consenting, correctly-recorded trade possible on the v8 world, such that:

- the traded goods are real and conserved (every unit accounted, no minting, no hidden subsidy);
- both sides gain for a reason the model can state (heterogeneous endowments or needs that the density measurement will have quantified);
- the recording discipline of the earlier tickets holds at full strength: independent intentions on both sides, no private-state leak, a typed atomic settlement, journal linkage that replay can verify, and rejection rather than silent partial settlement;
- the world stays bounded and starvation/collapse stay reachable (no new positive-feedback loop without a natural bound);
- the mechanism is attributable: the ticket names the single new mechanism and predeclares the band whose outcome would falsify the claim that trade occurred and mattered.

## Design questions (must be answered with exact numbers)

1. **What is traded, and what is the double coincidence?** Weigh and reject with reasons: (a) food-for-labour, i.e. a typed work contract where a food-rich actor pays granary stock for another actor's gathered unit or build labour (a wage in embryo); (b) a plot/tenure market where cultivated slots can be transferred (rent or sale) — noting that this immediately touches the frozen possession-by-identity rule and the all-tenure-holders land identity; (c) a **new durable, transferable good** (tools) that a holder can make and trade for food, giving a genuine two-good economy and a destination for money later; (d) credit/debt with deferred settlement (the operator's later rung, which needs enforcement or reputation and therefore richer motives).
2. **Consent and settlement semantics:** what is the offer/accept protocol in the frozen hourly lattice (which phase instants, how many per actor-hour, how it composes with gather/build/birth), and what makes settlement atomic in one kernel batch with zero-mutation refusal otherwise? Note the lessons already learned: a policy's consent floor can differ from the contract's, a policy refusal must be respected at the policy level, and a partial settlement path corrupts ledgers.
3. **Conservation identities:** the exact extensions (or the proof that no frozen identity changes), including what happens to `birthGranaryPaid`, the granary ledger's `provisioned`/`spoiled` terms, the inventory intake identity `gathered + provisioned = consumed + held + invested`, and the land identity if tenure can move.
4. **Why does either side accept?** State the in-world reading for each rule and each threshold (no constant chosen because it makes trade appear); say what the measurement from ticket 19 must show for the design's premise to hold.
5. **What is deferred, and what would the money rung need?** State explicitly what this ticket does not do (credit, money, prices, institutions) and what evidence a later money design would need (e.g. a fungible good with a stable exchange ratio and repeated trade).
6. **Interaction with reproduction and the phase boundary:** does trade interact with the birth gate, the generational concentration of surplus, or the mouths-vs-slots oscillator? What must stay frozen so the v5–v8 evidence remains valid controls?
7. **Falsifiers:** what result would show that the exchange mechanism did not in fact produce trade (e.g. zero filings of offers, offers only ever accepted by one side, no measurable redistribution), and what would show it distorted the ecology rather than operating within it?

## Required designer outputs

Chosen mechanism with exact constants and in-world readings; rejected alternatives with reasons; the new version identity (world/policy axes) and its honest cost; predeclared bands with numeric thresholds and falsification criteria (including a null-trade band); the experiment matrix and the single-variable ladder, referencing the ticket-19 phase map for cell selection; metric definitions; lane board; stop conditions. If the honest conclusion is that exchange cannot be made meaningful with the goods the v8 world has, say so with the evidence and name what must be added first — do not invent a trade-flavoured mechanism that nothing has a reason to use.

## Constraints

- v1–v8 world bytes, policies, checkpoints and all recorded evidence stay frozen; v9 is a new version, never a re-tune.
- No post-hoc tuning; a null result (no trade occurs, or trade occurs without effect) is valid and must be reportable verbatim.
- Determinism, replay and checkpoint/branch discipline unchanged; compute ceilings stay instrumented with the P15 verdict table.
- Any transferable good must respect the conservation ledger and the privacy discipline (a trader must never see the counterparty's private state beyond what the counter-offer protocol publishes).

## Frozen design: v9 = the wage contract (mechanism version 7 + policy natural/9)

**Chosen trade and its double coincidence.** One contract type only: a landless worker delivers one gathered wild unit into an employer's worksite (`wip +1`) and the employer pays exactly **one granary stock unit** as wage — a derived **1:1** rate, no price field, no haggling. Employer side: surplus stock above the sustain point is unusable wealth on a 24-hour rot clock (intake is capped at ≈2 meals/h while banks measured up to 96 units), and a contract converts one rotting stock unit into one `wip` at **zero employer-hours**, whereas self-provisioning (rule 515) spends the identical unit **plus one build-hour**; wages therefore buy time, and time is the binding constraint on capital formation (own-labour cap 0.5 points/h gives k\*≈3; one contract per hour doubles the formation rate and lifts k\* toward ≈6), with wear (one point per 6/k hours) structurally reopening demand so trade recurs. Worker side: the wage is **the first landless granary-inflow channel in the world's history** (rule 508 requires tenure, rule 515 requires existing stock), and the worker's outside option (gather-then-eat) is exactly net zero, so 1 delivered unit for 1 stock unit leaves the worker whole on food while converting the hour into either immediate survival (drawn at E≤3 by the frozen banking floor) or banking toward the `granary ≥ 3` birth gate that is otherwise unreachable without land; when tenure-saturated hours empty the wild commons the outside option is starvation and the wage is strictly better.

**Rejected alternatives (with reasons).** (b) *Plot/tenure market*: tenure writes are admitted by G5 only to rules 503/505/514 and the land identity binds each tenured slot to an owner homed on that patch, while the per-actor capital ledgers (`invested = 2·created + wip`, `created − decayed = k`) carry no transfer terms — a sale would require re-attributing created/invested, i.e. ledger corruption by construction; it would also convert k into transferable wealth and silently change what the frozen self-reported `k ≥ 2` birth claims mean, and it preinstalls a market in the strongest production asset (P10) where no buyer exists (land is free on the under-capacity patch and worthless on the saturated one). (c) *A new durable good (tools)*: every use-value variant re-tunes a frozen constant (gather/build yield, wear 6/k, spoilage 24) and would invalidate the ticket-19 phase map the cell selection rests on, while a use-value-free tool has no acceptance reason at this rung — that is the money problem, not the exchange problem; named as the money rung's prerequisite instead (§5). (d) *Credit/debt*: no enforcement, no reputation channel, memoryless policies, and S=24 spoilage with 96–143 h lifetimes makes intertemporal settlement hazardous (debtors die with no estate); deferred verbatim per the operator's sequencing (reciprocity after affect/motive richness). **Parent decision 2 (confirmed): the base policy for natural/9 is natural/7b** — the strongest arm (banking-only flips A2 in every v8 gating cell) and the clean single-variable ladder; basing it on natural/7 would confound the counterproductive provisioning channel into the trade step.

**Protocol in the frozen lattice (no new phase instants; the 515 precedent).** `h+40m+5µs` rule **516 labour-offer** (worker files an addressed offer on its own offer-book row, component `0x80000058`, carrying only hour/addressee/status/last-offer-hour/last-reply-hour — the birth-request field set minus the self-reported claims, terms fixed; preconditions alive, bag=0, no pending offer, last-offer-hour < h, same-patch addressee). `h+40m+6µs` rule **517 labour-decision** (employer accepts or refuses pending offers addressed to it, ascending worker ID, one decision per actor-hour; accept writes the worker's offer row — the 511 enumerated-two-party precedent — and its own engagement row on component `0x80000059`; refusal writes status rows only). `h+40m+3µs` rule **518 labour-settlement** (the atomic settlement, caused by the worker, in **one** kernel proposal: worker bag −1, worker granary stock +1, worker `wagesEarned` +1, employer granary stock −1, employer `wagesPaid` +1, employer `invested` +1 and `wip` +1 — and when `wip` reaches 2 the frozen point completion fires **including the lowest-index-free-wild-slot claim with stock 0 through the same ascending-cause-ID fold as rule 505 completions**, so an employer completing its own build in the same hour never collides and multiple settlements serialize deterministically). Next pulse rule **519 labour-expiry/void** (unanswered offers expire, void contracts clear — the 513 discipline verbatim). **Composition and budgets:** one gather plus one build **or one delivery** per actor-hour (the delivery retargets the worker's unit conversion), and one negotiation filing (birth-request or labour-offer) plus one negotiation decision (birth-reply or labour-decision) per actor-hour; a worker with a pending contract gathers at the claim under the frozen cascade and emits a new `NaturalDeliver` action at the bag decision (enum appended, no `String()` — the 515 ruling); a denied gather fails and voids the settlement with a typed cause. **Fresh-snapshot revalidation** at settlement: contract pending ∧ both alive ∧ same patch ∧ worker bag=1 ∧ employer stock ≥1 ∧ employer `k+wip<8` ∧ free-slot availability if a point completes; any failure is a typed void with zero resource mutation, the worker's bag intact, and the worker re-entering the documented denial path (`LastDenial = ContractDenied` → re-evaluate → Eat) — the build-denial loop verbatim. There is no two-phase path: the wage can never land without the paired `wip +1`.
**The three already-paid-for lessons, applied explicitly:** a policy consent floor may differ from the contract's and **a policy refusal is final, resolved at the policy level with a typed reason, no execution, no draws and no candidate** (the E12 ruling) while a policy accept that the world refuses voids with zero mutation — never scratch-execution-then-override; **partial settlement corrupts ledgers**, so settlement is a single folded enumerated proposal in one batch; and the identity mismatch checks run **before any restore work or branch publication** with publish-nothing verified over {5,6,7}.

**Conservation.** Additive and mechanism-7-only: granary fields 8/9 `wages-paid`/`wages-earned` [0,168] (horizon envelopes, no behaviour-binding cap); **G2a″** per actor `cultivatedReceived + wagesEarned = stock + storedMeals + provisioned + spoiled + birthGranaryPaid + wagesPaid` (term-for-term reducible to G2a′ under v5/v6 where wages ≡ 0); G5 admissions for rules 516/517/518/519 exactly as enumerated (possession-by-identity is breached **only** by that one settlement channel, and tenure mode/owner remain writable only by 503/505/514). **Unchanged with reasons:** G1 per patch-hour `gathered + provisioned = consumed + held + invested` holds because the delivered unit is `gathered` (worker) and `invested` (employer) inside the same closed patch, and trade is same-patch-only so the per-patch closure (the Gate-3 discipline) is preserved; G3 moves no energy (the wage meal is a normal eat-stored draw and `birthGranaryPaid` is untouched); G2b holds incrementally (`invested +1, wip +1`, or `wip −1, created +1` on completion, satisfying `invested = 2·created + wip`); the land identity holds because tenure never moves.

**Derived thresholds (no constant tuned).** Wage = **1:1** — the worker's outside option prices any delivered unit at ≥1 and the employer's outside option (frozen 1:1 provision) at ≤1, so the unique bilateral-surplus rate is 1. Accept floor **stock ≥ 4 = R\*+2**, the same derivation reused verbatim from frozen TProvision/TFile ("pay only from reserve plus the full price; ≥3 = R\*+1 remains"), so below 4 the stock is survival stock, not wage stock. Accept requires **`k+wip < 8`** (frozen KMax), so wages buy wip only where capital formation is legal and wear periodically reopens demand.
